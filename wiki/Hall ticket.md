<h1 align="center">Hall Tickets: When, What, Where?</h1>

---

## 🎫 Hall Ticket
[Visit this link](https://sdmis.nios.ac.in/search/hall-ticket) and enter your enrollment number to get your hall ticket.

---

## 📘 PCP
- Non-compulsory classes  
- Hall ticket is available on your dashboard  
- If not available, no worries  

---

## 📘 FA PCP Hall Ticket
- Compulsory practical subject classes  
- Carries 50% of practical marks  
- Hall ticket is released very late  

**Important**
- Do not call or email your study center  
- Visit your study center directly  
- Ask for the exact date and time of classes  
- Carry your **ID card** if hall ticket is not available  

---

## 📅 General Dates

### Stream 1 Block 1 April Exam
- Hall ticket available between **1 March to 10 March**

### Stream 1 Block 2 October Exam
- Hall ticket available between **1 September to 10 September**

---

## 🧪 Practical Exam / SA PCP

- Hall ticket available **1 to 2 weeks before practical exam**

### Stream 1 Block 1 April Exam
- Between **5 March to 15 March**

### Stream 1 Block 2 October Exam
- Between **5 September to 15 September**

---

## 📝 Public Exam
- Hall ticket available **1 to 2 weeks before exam starts**

---

## 🖥️ ODE Hall Ticket

### Stream 3 / 4
- When admission is completed, hall ticket is generated  
- Available in the **ODE section**

### ODE Exam for One or More Subjects
- Book seat  
- Pay fees  
- Instant hall ticket available in **ODE section**

---

## 🏫 Important
- Practical and FA PCP are held at your **study center**  
- Read the **Study Center Guide** to know more
