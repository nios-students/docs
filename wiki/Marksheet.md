# 🕐 Result & Issuance Timeline
• Online results: ~40 to 45 days after exams.
• DigiLocker mark­sheets: ~2 weeks after result.
• Physical copies: another ~30–45 days after results.
## (If it is urgent, you can visit your regional centre or NIOS HQ for faster collection)

# 📍 Where to Collect
• **Public exam** → After 40 to 45 days at your Study Centre or Regional Centre
• **ODE exam** → 40–45 days (home delivery or regional centre)
### Call NIOS HQ or RC  if you haven't received your marksheet yet in the given timeline.

---

# 📌 Certification Criteria
You must fulfill certification criteria (enough subjects passed) to get your marksheet & passing certificate. Otherwise, the Marksheet will show **XXXX** in place of **“PASS."**.

---

# 📥 Marksheets for Part Admission
If you take less than 5 subjects, You will have to apply for Part Admission Marksheet from your **NIOS Account - E service**.
 
You’ll only get a marksheet with **XXXX**, and no passing certificate.
