## 🟦 AFTER ADMISSION — WHAT NEXT?

**Admission is a two-step process.**

---

## 1️⃣ Document Verification  

If you filled everything correctly and properly
The **NIOS Regional Centre ** verifies your documents.
Your Admission Status will get **Admission verified**

- **Stream 1:** ~1 week to 2 months (depends on workload)  
- **Stream 2 / 3 / 4:** usually within 1–2-3 weeks  

👉 Keep checking **Admission Status** in your NIOS student account.

---

### 2️⃣ Admission Confirmation  
After verification:
- You will receive an email!
- Admission gets **confirmed**
- You receive your **Enrollment Number**
- **ID Card** becomes available online  
  *(Print and laminate it)*

---

### 📢 Important Note  
If NIOS finds any issue in your documents:
- A **correction notice** appears on the dashboard and email too
- You can **re-upload** the required document  

This is why checking admission status regularly is critical.

---

At this point, you are officially a **NIOS student**.  
But admission is only the beginning.

You still have:
- TMA  
- Practicals  
- Exam fees  
- Exam timelines


**so yeah after admission, there will be only one more payment **exam fees** and  you just need to keep yourself updated with your NIOS stream block updates (don't miss on things).**
