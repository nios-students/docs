
# 🟦💠 AFTER ADMISSION, WHAT NEXT? :

## IT's a two step process

First - your document will get **verified** by the NIOS regional center. this may take 1 week to 2 month (for stream 1 learners) depending on your luck and their workload & usually within a week or two for stream 2/3/4 learners. Keep checking Admission Status••• through your NIOS Student dashboard Account.

2nd : After Your documents have been verified, your admission will  get **confirmed** in few days and you will be assigned a enrollment number and ID card online ( which you should print and laminate).

📢 NOTE : If there is any inconsistency in your document, NIOS will ask for correction ( through a notification on dashboard) and you'll be able to re-upload that document. This is why keeping check on admission status is crucial. Other Wise you will miss the issue notification and won't get admission done under deadline!
