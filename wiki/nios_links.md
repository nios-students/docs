# NIOS & SDMIS Links

## 1. Core Student Services

1. **SDMIS Student Portal (Main Dashboard)**  
   https://sdmis.nios.ac.in/  
   Central portal for all student-related services including admissions, exams, hall tickets, and notifications.

2. **SDMIS Login Page**  
   https://sdmis.nios.ac.in/auth  
   Login page for enrolled students to access their dashboard.

3. **NIOS Results Portal**  
   https://results.nios.ac.in/  
   Official portal to check NIOS public examination results.

4. **Hall Ticket – Public Examinations**  
   https://sdmis.nios.ac.in/search/hall-ticket  
   Download admit card for theory and practical public examinations.

5. **FA Hall Ticket (Formative Assessment)**  
   https://sdmis.nios.ac.in/search/fa-hall-ticket  
   Download admit card for Formative Assessment examinations.
