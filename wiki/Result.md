# When Are NIOS Results Declared?

[NIOS official result website](https://results.nios.ac.in/)

---

Source of information: past trends, experience, and NIOS prospectus

## Stream 1 – Block 1 (April–May Exam)

**12th Class**
- Result: mid-June (usually 1–2 days before JoSAA counselling)
- [Important note on JoSAA & late NIOS results](https://www.reddit.com/r/Nios_unofficial/comments/1lg1z90/late_results_aftermath_in_jeejosaa_2025_important/)

**10th Class**
- Result: end of June

**Documents**
- DigiLocker marksheet & certificates: ~2 weeks after result
- [Guide: NIOS DigiLocker documents](https://www.reddit.com/r/Nios_unofficial/comments/1lf7f0i/a_guide_on_issue_of_marksheet_certificates_how_to/)
- Physical marksheet & certificates: end of July to August (via study centre)

---

## Stream 1 – Block 2 & Stream 2 (Oct–Nov Exam)

**12th Class**
- Result: mid-January

**10th Class**
- Result: end of January

**Documents**
- DigiLocker: ~2 weeks after result
- Physical documents: around March (visit study centre then)

---

## ODE Exams (Feb–March / July–Sept)

- Results are released of every cycle ~15 days after **45–50 days**
  (sometimes delayed to 55–60 days)
- Example: Results  of 1–15 Feb →  then 16–29 Feb

[Past ODE result trends](https://shorturl.at/nCkQ2)

- Full result is visible **only after your last exam**
  (e.g., one exam in Feb + one in March → final full result in March cycle)

**Documents**
- DigiLocker: ~2 weeks after result
- Physical marksheet: 30–45 days (home delivery or regional centre)

For faster collection, visit your regional centre or NIOS HQ.
