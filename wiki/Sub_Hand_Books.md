## NIOS Mathematics Study Order (Based on NCERT Sequence)

As you know, the NIOS Mathematics syllabus is a mix of Class 11 and Class 12 topics.  
To make studying easier with CBSE teachers on YouTube, I reorganized the chapters according to the **NCERT order** and separated them into **Class 11 and Class 12 level topics**.

---

### Class 11 Topics (NIOS Chapter Numbers)

Study these first:

13, 14, 15, 16, 33, 25 *(Limits & Continuity)*, 38

---

### Class 12 Topics (NCERT Order — NIOS Chapter Numbers)

23, 22, 20, 24, 21, 26, 27, 28, 29, 30, 31, 32, 37

---

### Important Note

- **Chapter 25 (Limits & Continuity)** and **Chapter 38 (Mathematical Reasoning)** are **not from NCERT Standard Mathematics**.
- These topics come from **Applied Mathematics (Class 11)**.

So when searching for lectures on YouTube, search specifically:

**limit and continuity applied mathematics class 11**

---

### Why I Made This Order

- I arranged the chapters this way so that I can **follow CBSE teachers on YouTube easily**, since most of them teach according to the **NCERT sequence**.

- Now All you have to do is study these chapters from CBSE teachers (Next topper, PW, sci and fun , magnet brains or anyone you like)

- Target these chapters with NIOS module marks weigtage wise as given on their site!

- Then do nios textbook / pdf questions side by side, then NIOS terminal questions, then buri nazar on NIOS PQYS, and question banks!
