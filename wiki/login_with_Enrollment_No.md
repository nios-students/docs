# **How to Recover Access to Your NIOS Student Dashboard Using Only Your Enrollment Number**

## **Problem**

Many NIOS students face this situation:

- You **don't remember your registered email address**.
- You **don't remember your username/reference number**.
- You **forgot your password**.
- Your admission was completed through a **broker, coaching center, or another person**, and they only gave you your **Enrollment Number**, which is printed on your NIOS Identity Card.
- Previously, you always logged in using **Enrollment Number + Date of Birth**, but NIOS has now removed that login option.

If any of the above applies to you, don't worry. **You can still recover access to your NIOS dashboard using only your Enrollment Number.**

---

# Why This Happens

Earlier, students could sign in to the NIOS Student Dashboard using:

- **Enrollment Number + Date of Birth**

However, NIOS has changed its login system.

The dashboard now only allows login using one of the following:

- **Reference Number + Date of Birth**
- **Application Number + Date of Birth**
- **Registered Email + Password**

Because of this change, many students who only know their Enrollment Number are unable to log in.

Fortunately, there is still a simple way to retrieve your **Reference Number**.

---

# Step-by-Step Solution

## Step 1: Open the NIOS Admission Status Page

Go to the official [NIOS Admission Status](https://sdmis.nios.ac.in/registration/check-admission-status) website.

This page allows students to check their admission details.

---

## Step 2: Select "Enrollment Number"

You will see multiple search options.

Choose:

**Enrollment Number**

---

## Step 3: Enter Your Enrollment Number

Type your Enrollment Number exactly as printed on your NIOS Identity Card.

Then click **Submit**.

---

## Step 4: Open Your Admission Status

If the Enrollment Number is correct, the website will display your admission details.

You will see several sections containing information about your admission.

---

## Step 5: Find Your Reference Number

Look for the **Admission Details** section.

Inside this section, you will find:

- Course
- Other admission information
- **Reference Number**

The **Reference Number** is usually listed near the top of the Admission Details section.

Make a note of this number.

---

# Step 6: Login to the NIOS Student Dashboard

Now go to the official NIOS Student Dashboard.

Instead of using your Enrollment Number, select the login option:

**Reference Number + Date of Birth**

Enter:

- Reference Number (obtained from the Admission Status page)
- Date of Birth

You should now be able to access your dashboard.

---

# After Successfully Logging In

Once you have access to your account, it is highly recommended that you update your account information.

You should:

- Change your password to something memorable.
- Verify or update your registered email address.
- Save your Reference Number somewhere safe.
- Keep a backup of your login credentials.

This will help you avoid login problems in the future.

---

# Important Note

Your **Enrollment Number** is **not** your login credential anymore.

It is primarily used for:

- Identity verification
- Examination purposes
- Result checking
- Admission status lookup

To access the Student Dashboard, you now need one of the following:

- Reference Number + Date of Birth
- Application Number + Date of Birth
- Registered Email + Password

---

# Tips for the Future

To avoid losing access again:

- Save your Reference Number in a secure place.
- Store your registered email address and password in a password manager or notebook.
- Keep a screenshot or PDF of your Admission Details page.
- If someone else completed your admission, ask them for all login credentials immediately instead of only the Enrollment Number.

---

## Conclusion

Even if you only have your **Enrollment Number** and **Date of Birth**, you are **not locked out permanently**.

By visiting the **NIOS Admission Status** page, searching with your **Enrollment Number**, and retrieving your **Reference Number**, you can log back into the NIOS Student Dashboard and update all your account details.

This is one of the easiest ways to regain access when your email, password, or other login credentials have been forgotten.
