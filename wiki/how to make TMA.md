## What is TMA?

- **TMA (Tutor Marked Assignment)**, also known as **Internal Assessment (IA)** in Secondary/Sr. Secondary examinations, are written assignments that carry **20% weightage** of the theory marks as part of continuous and comprehensive evaluation.
- These marks will be reflected in the **mark-sheet** alongside the **theory marks** of the external examination, which carry **80% weightage** (of theory) in each subject.
- **Side Note**: TMA is applicable **only for Stream 1**.
- TMA marking scheme [see here](https://www.reddit.com/r/Nios_unofficial/comments/1l8jzfi/nios_marking_scheme_with_tma_adjustment_practical/).
- you can also check this reddit post for more info [here](https://www.reddit.com/r/Nios_unofficial/comments/1ljz4b4/simple_guide_on_tma/?share_id=frFdYYb5bWT2EEag5U_7E&utm_content=1&utm_medium=android_app&utm_name=androidcss&utm_source=share&utm_term=1)

## Meaning of TMA terms and the date of feedback of TMA
- TMA Submission pending: TMA isn't upload yet*
- Upload online: TMA is upload on the NIOS server*
- TMA Allocated: When the study centre gets your TMA(s) and accepts it
- TMA Evaluation in progress: The teacher is checking
- TMA Evaluated: Your TMA is corrected/check*
- "-": You passed in the subject

> [!NOTE] 
> Whatever is marked with “*” are important. The dates can differ from student to student but it takes ton of time for April 2024 the feedback/marks were given to everyone by the 31 April 2024.

## Key Notes

- ⚠️ **Assignments should be submitted before appearing in the Public Examination of the particular subject.**
- 📢 **AFTER DEADLINE, ₹1500 late fee will be taken for TMA upload (per subject).**
- 📢 **TMA facility is only available for STREAM 1 students. TMA is not applicable to learners seeking registration under Streams II, III & IV.**
- 📃 **Assignments should be submitted before appearing in the Public Examination of the particular subject. Assignments will not be accepted after passing the subject.**
- 📃 **The result of those learners who do not submit the Assignments is declared on the basis of Public Examination only.**
- 📃 **In case a learner uploads incorrect TMA, they can re-upload the correct TMA within seven days from the date of uploading incorrect TMA.**
- 📃 **Re-checking and Re-evaluation is not available in TMA.**
- 📢 **If a learner misses the TMA submission deadline, they can still submit it with a late fee of ₹1500 per subject (online only) after due date AND within one month from the result declaration date of the April-May or Oct-Nov exams.**
- 📢 **TMA once uploaded are valid for your whole academic journey in NIOS, no need to upload again.**
- 📢 **No improvement will be allowed in the TMA marks & Assignments will not be accepted after passing the subject.**
