### How to Change Your Study Centre (Simple Explanation)

If you want to change your study centre, it usually happens for one main reason: **your current study centre is inactive or asking for a bribe!**.

This means:
- You selected the study centre at the time of admission  
- But now, when you called or visited them, they told you they are **no longer affiliated with NIOS**
- or bribe case

  **You can either visit your Regional centre to change your study centre or do this, both are the same thing!**

---

### What to do in this case?

#### 1. Write a self-declaration letter (handwritten)
- Address it to **NIOS Headquarters** and **your NIOS Regional Centre**
- Mention:
  - Your **name**
  - Your **enrolment number**
- **Subject:**  
  *Request to change study centre as the current one is inactive*

#### 2. Write the body (keep it short: 2–3 lines)
- You visited or contacted your current study centre  
- They / Principle informed you that they are inactive / no longer affiliated with NIOS  
- Due to this, you want to change your study centre  
- Mention the **new study centre you want to shift to**
- just chat gpt the format bro
- [Here is one Self-Decl.. letter Example](/wiki/assets/Self del for bribe center.jpeg)

#### 3. Choose the new study centre carefully
- Make sure the study centre is **active**
- Confirm it is **currently affiliated with NIOS**
- Do not repeat the same mistake
  How to do it? here is the NIOS SC portal! - https://sdmis.nios.ac.in/registration/locate-study-center

#### 4. Complete the self-declaration
- Write your **name**
- Add your **signature** at the end

#### 5. Apply through e-Services
- Go to the **NIOS e-Services portal**
- Apply for **Change of Study Centre**
- Upload:
  - Your **handwritten self-declaration**
  - Your **Aadhaar card**
- Merge both documents into **one single image** before uploading

---
**Small tip: After applying for a change, also email lsc@nios.ac.in and [your Regional Centre](https://nios-unofficial.info/wiki/chat/#official-nios-contact-information)that you applied for this, attach your payment receipt. And Please approve this request!**

That’s it. Follow the steps properly and the request should go through without issues and will be confirmed in week or 2.
