# E-services
You can use e-services to change, edit and add information. Basically this saves your butt if you screwed up!
## How does this work?
You select the service you want apply for attach documents and  pay the fees. Your [RC](/wiki/Glossary) will verify it then forward it to [SSS](/wiki/Glossary) they will confirm it.

### How to Change Your Study Centre (Simple Explanation)

If you want to change your study centre, it usually happens for one main reason: **your current study centre is inactive or asking for a bribe!**.

This means:
- You selected the study centre at the time of admission  
- But now, when you called or visited them, they told you they are **no longer affiliated with NIOS**
- or bribe case

### TBH, you don’t need to lose your mind if your NIOS study centre is INACTIVE

Here’s why:
- If your study centre is inactive, the **NIOS system automatically updates your details** for practical exams
- When the **practical exam schedule is released**, the system **assigns a new practical centre** on your hall ticket
- The new centre is usually **near your location, within the same district**
- Your **practical hall ticket** which comes 1 week before practical exam will clearly show:
  - The new practical centre  
  - Practical exam date and details  

This works as a built-in **safety mechanism**, so students are not affected even if the original study centre is no longer active.

So if you’re thinking, *“What will happen to my exam?”* — here’s the simple answer:
- Your practical exam will **automatically shift to a new centre**
- All updated details will appear on your **new practical hall ticket**

### Okay, but what about my TMA marks?
- You just need to **upload your TMAs properly**
- The study centre being inactive does **not affect TMA evaluation**
- NIOS HQ or RC will automatically give TMA marks on your result!

## NIOS 12th Admission Issue: Picked Part Admission "Yes" and Previous qualification "12th Passed" by Mistake (How to Fix It)! 
<details>
<summary>📘 12th Part Admission "Yes", Previous qualtifical "12th" and TOC issue (How to Fix Guide)!.</summary>

   ### You wanted to take admission in NIOS 12th as a fresher with 5 subjects, but you picked these options
   **Part Admission = YES** + **Previous Qualification = 12th Passed** or **Opted for TOC too as a 12th passed student when you TOC option is only for failed students**
   and now you only have **3 or 4 subjects max**!
   
   
   ---
   
   So first, understand the mess you are in:
   - The **Part Admission** option is meant for people who are **already 12th passed** and later in life want to add **1 or 2 subjects** (like Maths, Physics, etc.) for things like pilot programs, navy, or similar requirements.
   - TOC option is for failed students, not for passed students!
   
   
   ### What went wrong
   
   
   You took admission in NIOS **without properly reading how it works or which stream/option is meant for what purpose**, and now you’re panicking and blaming NIOS for your **mistake**.
   
   
   Let’s be clear:
   Yeah — **you messed up**. Hard.
   
   
   But relax.
   **This mistake can be fixed**, *if* you follow the steps carefully from here.
   No more random clicks. No more dumb moves.
 
  
  
  
  ---
  
  
  ### There are 2 cases, so the solution for each is different
  
    (click button to open)
   
   <details>
   ### <summary>📘 CASE 1 - Part admission "Yes", Previous qualification "12th",  but NO TOC!.</summary>
 
   ### Case 1
 
 
   You applied for **Class 12 admission** and selected:
   - **Previous Qualification:** 12th Passed
   - **Part Admission:** YES
   - **No TOC**, no TOC
   
   
   Check your **NIOS dashboard → Application Status**, you will see:
   - these two options
   
   
   👉 **Good news:** you are still safe.
   This setup **can be reversed, and fast**.
   
   
   ---
   
   
   #### ✅ Solution (Follow carefully)
   
   
   #### Step 1: Change Previous Qualification
   
   
   1. Log in to your **NIOS account**
   2. Go to **e-Services**
   3. Apply for **Previous Qualification Change**
   4. Change:
   - From **12th Passed**
   - To **10th Passed**
   
   #### Document to upload (important)
   
   
   Write a **simple handwritten letter**. Content like this:
   
   
   > To
   > NIOS Headquarters and Regional Centre
   >
   > Name: _Your Name_
   > Enrollment No: _Your Enrollment Number_
   >
   > Dear Sir/Madam,
   > I am applying for a previous qualification change from **12th passed to 10th passed**, and for changing **Part Admission from YES to NO** in my NIOS account.
   > This was done due to my mistake while taking admission.
   > I want to correct it so that I can take **5 subjects** and make a **full admission** in NIOS.
   > Please kindly approve my request and help me correct my mistake.
   >
   > Thank you
   > Signature
   
   
   - Merge this **handwritten letter + Aadhaar card** into **one image**
   - Upload it as proof
   
   
   ---
   
   
   #### Step 2: Change Part Admission to NO
   
   
   1. Again go to **e-Services**
   2. Apply for **Part Admission Change**
   3. Change:
   - From **YES**
   - To **NO**
   4. Upload the **same merged document** (letter + Aadhaar)
   
   
   #### If by chance the Part Admission window is not working, like not opening then do this or if working the go to step 3
   
   
   - Email these **together**:
   - Your **LSC**
   - Your **Regional Centre**
   - NIOS (Dir/Support)
   - Clearly mention that the **Part Admission change option is not working**
   
   
   ---
   
   
   #### Step 3: NOW wait (don’t panic)
   - This whole thing will take 2 to 3 weeks! so wait , fking wait
   - Eservice thing is a **2-step process**:
   - First: your request is **verified** (accepted)
   - Then: it is **approved / confirmed** (changes are applied)
   - Keep checking:
   - A **notification** in your dashboard
   - Or the values will **change directly**
   
   
   
   
   **Pro tip:**
   Once you’ve applied for everything, you can also **politely email your LSC / RC** with your details and payment slip to speed up the approval process.
   Do **not** spam.
   
   
   ---
   
   
   #### 🔴 Important Final Step 4 (Most people forget this)
   
   
   Once **both changes are approved and visible**:
   
   
   - Go to **e-Services**
   - Add **1 or 2 subjects** (to make a total of **5 subjects**)
   - Subjects get added **almost instantly** (1–2 minutes)
   
   
   ---
   
   
   #### 💸 Fees Breakdown
   
   
   - Previous Qualification Change: **₹200**
   - Part Admission Change: **₹200**
   - Add Subject: **₹940 per subject**
   
   
   ---
   
   
   #### ✅ Final Result
   
   
   And that’s it — now you are done.
   You now have **5 subjects in NIOS**, *only if you did everything properly*.
   
   
   - Full admission restored
   - 5 subjects allowed
   - You’re back on track
   
   
   That’s it.
   You messed up — but **you’re saved** if you do this properly.
