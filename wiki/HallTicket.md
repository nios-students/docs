<h1 align="center">Hall Tickets: When, What, Where?</h1>

---

## 🎫 Hall Ticket
This is where your hall ticket will be available when it is released! [Visit this link](https://sdmis.nios.ac.in/search/hall-ticket) and enter your enrollment number to get your hall ticket.

---

## 📘 PCP
- Non-compulsory classes  
- Not Hall ticket, but the schedule  is generally  available on your dashboard  when the time comes
- If not available, no worries  

---

## 📘 FA PCP Hall Ticket
- Compulsory practical subject classes  
- Carries 50% of practical marks  
- Hall ticket is released very late  

**Important**
- Do not call or email your study centre  
- Visit your study centre directly  when the time comes
- Ask for the exact date and time of classes, cuz only they will give you the exact date and time, NIOS only gives a general timeline
- Carry your **ID card** if hall ticket is not available

  **If your center is inactive and you want to know what to do, where to visit for FA or SA PCP class, [Then click here](https://nios-unofficial.info/wiki/EServices#basic-details-change-correction)**

---

## 📅 General Dates For FA PCP HALL Ticket

### Stream 1 Block 1 April Exam
- Hall ticket available between **1 March to 10 March**

### Stream 1 Block 2 October Exam
- Hall ticket available between **1 September to 10 September**

---

## 🧪 Practical Exam / SA PCP

- Hall ticket available **1 to 2 weeks before practical exam**. Always on time not late!

### SA PCP HALL Ticket for Stream 1 Block 1 April Exam
- Between **5 March to 15 March**

### SA PCP HALL Ticket for Stream 1 Block 2 or Stream 2 October Exam
- Between **5 September to 15 September**

---

## 📝 Public Exam Hall ticket
- Hall ticket available **1 to 2 weeks before exam starts**

---

## Public Exam Date Sheet 
- 2 to 3 weeks before exam starts

---

## 🖥️ ODE Hall Ticket

### Stream 3 / 4
- When admission is confirmed, the hall ticket is generated  at the same time
- Available in the **ODE section**
- No Extra Hall ticket for practical exams tho.
-  Hall ticket for practicals is the same as theory, ask your teachers at the exam centre for the exact date!

### If You Are Booking An ODE Exam Seat Separately or Again for One or More Subjects
- Book a seat  
- Pay fees  
- Instant hall ticket available in **ODE section**
